# Sources of the cloud and night-light layers

Placed by Claude Code on 30 October 2026 for the look pass. The surface textures' source is in `SOURCE.md`.

## cloud_combined_8192.tif

- **What:** NASA's Blue Marble cloud layer, clouds only, as white on black: global, 8192 x 4096 pixels (about 5 km
  per pixel), RGB TIFF, equirectangular (plate carree), longitude -180 to 180 left to right, north up. A composite
  of two days of MODIS visible imagery, with a third day of thermal infrared over the poles (from the 2002 Blue
  Marble). It is the largest single global map NASA offers: the 21600-pixel versions come as two hemisphere tiles,
  43200 pixels wide together.
- **Source:** NASA Earth Observatory, Visible Earth record 57747 (Blue Marble: Clouds), downloaded 30 October 2026 from
  https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57747/cloud_combined_8192.tif
  (35,870,468 bytes, sha256:d137775d8966ab8d443fd5126dc6e7ad72072bc1ed50555c5818d221735daf0f).
- **Made by:** NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Image by Reto Stockli (land surface, shallow water, clouds);
  enhancements by Robert Simmon. Data from the MODIS Science Team.
- **Terms:** NASA says these images are freely available to educators, scientists, museums and the public, and NASA
  imagery is generally not subject to copyright in the United States (NASA's media usage guidelines). It must not
  suggest NASA endorses the work. Credit: "NASA Goddard Space Flight Center; image by Reto Stockli". It carries
  no NASA insignia; do not add one.

## BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg

- **What:** NASA's Earth at Night (Black Marble) 2016 color map: global, 13500 x 6750 pixels (3 km per pixel),
  JPEG, equirectangular (plate carree), longitude -180 to 180 left to right, north up. City lights from the
  Suomi NPP satellite's VIIRS day-night band, composited over 2016. It is the largest single global map NASA
  offers: the 500 m version comes as eight 21600 x 21600 tiles, 86400 pixels wide together.
- **Source:** NASA Earth Observatory, Earth at Night flat maps
  (https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps), downloaded 30 October 2026 from
  https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg
  (8,106,233 bytes, sha256:230aac448ae68c358be433dd518888cccb3a85ccf66f7b44326441c324ad6725).
- **Made by:** NASA Earth Observatory images by Joshua Stevens, using Suomi NPP VIIRS data from Miguel Roman, NASA's
  Goddard Space Flight Center.
- **Terms:** as above: NASA imagery, generally not subject to copyright in the United States, free to use with
  credit and without suggesting NASA's endorsement. Credit: "NASA Earth Observatory / Joshua Stevens; Suomi NPP
  VIIRS data from Miguel Roman, NASA GSFC". It carries no NASA insignia; do not add one.

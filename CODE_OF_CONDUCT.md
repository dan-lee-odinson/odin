# Code of conduct

This is a small project directed by one person, the Director, in the open. These are the rules for taking part in it, on the issue tracker, in pull requests, and anywhere else the project is discussed.

## What is expected

- Be direct and be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until shown otherwise.
- Keep criticism specific: say what is wrong, where, and what would fix it.
- Respect that the Director decides design questions, and that a "no" is an answer, not an invitation to re-ask.
- Keep other people's private information out of the project.

## What is not tolerated

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity or circumstances.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption: re-opening settled decisions, flooding threads, or derailing issues.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to dan.lee.odinson@gmail.com. Reports are read by the Director, kept confidential as far as the situation allows, and answered within a week. The Director may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people from the project. A first incident usually gets a warning; a serious one, or a second one, gets a block. A decision can be appealed once, in writing, to the same address.

## Scope

These rules apply to every space the project runs (the repository, its issues and pull requests, and discussions about it that the Director takes part in) and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
